• I keep the hens for the town. Water in one trough, grain in the other, and they still squabble over both.
• Mind the gate when you pass. A hen that gets out takes half a morning to catch.
• If you see a trough run dry, never fear. I'll have it filled before they start complaining.
• A chick grows into a hen in half a year. I have watched more of them grow up than I can count.
• They go up the ladder at dusk all on their own. Wiser than some folk I know.
• Rain fills the water trough for me, but no cloud ever rained grain.
• Five hens, five tempers. I know each of them by her cluck.
