License for the Trade of Hardwoods
By the grace of the Town Council of Blackwater and the splinter-riddled, iron-grip oversight of the Order of Loggers, the bearer is hereby granted the legal right to fell, mill, and trade the sturdy bones of our ancient forests. While we require timber for hearth and hall, the bearer is strictly forbidden from passing off green, sap-weeping timber as "expertly seasoned oak". Furthermore, the merchant shall not stuff hollow logs with river-mud to tip the lumber-scales. Go forth and build the foundations of our realm, provided you do not clear-cut the Sacred Grove and bring a curse of dryads upon our gates.
