License for Stone Trading
By the grace of the Town Council of Blackwater and the somewhat sober oversight of the Masons’ Guild, the bearer—having finally graduated from the simple-minded business of peddling sticks and kindling—is hereby granted the legal right to buy, sell, and lug heavy chunks of the earth across our markets. While we recognize that trading stone requires significantly less intellect than wood, as rocks generally refuse to rot or catch fire, the bearer must still refrain from passing off common gravel as "pre-crushed castle walls" or selling "petrified bread" to the local garnison. Go forth and profit, provided you do not drop your inventory on the Mayor’s gouty foot or cause a localized landslide within the city walls.
