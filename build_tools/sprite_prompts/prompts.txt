==================== Wine trader (male) ====================

The attached picture is a split view. The left side shows a sprite of the main character from my game "Merchant's Rise", a medieval trading game. The right side is empty. Draw a new character in the empty right side.

The new character is a wine trader who sells wine at a medieval town market.

Match the original sprite exactly in:
- Style: the same chunky pixel art, pixel size, dark outline, flat shading and color depth. No anti-aliasing, gradients or blur.
- Proportions: the same chibi build, with a large head, big black rectangular eyes, a small body and short legs. The new character must be the same height and width as the original and sit at the same position in its panel.
- Posture: the same front-facing standing pose, with arms hanging at the sides and feet in the same place.
- Background: plain white, with no shadow, ground or scenery.

Make him clearly a different person from the player and clearly a wine trader:
- A rounder, well-fed build with rosy cheeks and a reddish nose, like someone who enjoys his own wine.
- Short dark curly hair or a receding hairline, with a short beard.
- A wine-red (burgundy) tunic under a long dark-green or cream apron that has a few small purple wine stains.
- A simple cloth cap or beret in a muted color.
- A small clay wine jug or a dark green glass bottle in one hand, held low at his side like the pouch in the original. The other hand can hang empty or hold a small wooden cup.
- Brown boots and dark trousers.

Keep the palette earthy and medieval, with burgundy, deep purple, dark green, brown and cream, so he fits the same game world as the original. Change only the right side and leave the left side exactly as it is. Output the full split image.
